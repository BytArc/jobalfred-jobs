# Data licence

The compiled lists in `data/` and `lists/` are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**You may** copy, share and adapt them, including commercially.

**You must** credit them. For example: "Job data from JobAlfred (github.com/BytArc/jobalfred-jobs), CC BY 4.0".

**What this covers:** our selection and arrangement of the data. Employers keep every right in their own names,
marks and posting text. Job titles and the short quotes on the sponsorship list come from employers' public postings
and are shown to identify the role. We claim no rights in them.

**Why CC BY rather than CC0:** the lists exist to help job seekers find this resource, and a credit keeps that path
open when the data is reused.
